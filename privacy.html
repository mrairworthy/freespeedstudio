Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes.

See GitHub Pages data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement for details of GitHub's processing, international transfers and privacy rights.

We use this hosting to deliver the website and maintain its security. Our legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in providing a reliable, secure website about our apps. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, third-party font services or tracking scripts. This site does not set its own cookies or store information in your browser.

When you contact us

Email links open your chosen email application. If you send us a message, we receive the email address and information you include so we can respond to your inquiry. Please share only the details needed for your request.

We use inquiry information to respond and deal with the matter you raise. For support related to an existing purchase or a request before entering a contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For general inquiries and feedback, it is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in responding to messages and supporting our products. We retain it only as needed for that purpose or to meet applicable retention obligations.

Links to other websites

Links to good2way.com, the App Store and GitHub take you to separate websites with their own privacy information. They are ordinary links; no third-party page is embedded here.
