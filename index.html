goodStats

For Mac · Version 1.0

Your Mac, at a glance.

CPU, memory and network readings in your menu bar. Open the native details panel for a closer look at your Mac, and choose what stays visible.

Explore goodStatsHelp & support

Coming to the Mac App Store

  • See the details. CPU, memory, disk, network, battery, thermal state and GPU hardware information.
  • Make the menu bar yours. Choose modules, order, labels and reading styles.
  • Keep it local. No account, ads, analytics or telemetry. System readings stay on your Mac.

Requires macOS 27 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.

good2way

For iPhone & iPad · Version 2.0.0

Aviation reference, close at hand.

An offline-focused FIR/UIR phrasebook for pilots, with search, favorites, personal notes and useful aviation reference tools.

Visit good2way.comView on the App Store
  • Explore FIRs and UIRs. Version 2.0.0 adds an interactive map with boundary outlines and search.
  • More reference tools. AIRAC cycle, Cold Temperature Correction, coordinate conversion with ARINC 424 formats, and an expanded glossary.
  • Your own workflow. Organize favorites into groups and keep personal notes nearby.

Free download with optional in-app purchases. Reference information only; current official aviation sources take precedence.
